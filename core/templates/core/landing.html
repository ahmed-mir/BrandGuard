Human-controlled, by design

The right person
has the final say.

Review can surface useful context. Approval belongs to an authorised person, for a specific version of the content.

01Evidence supports the decision.
02People resolve findings and approve.
03Only the approved version is ready to export.

If the content or approved policy changes later, that approval is no longer current. BrandGuard blocks export until the new version is reviewed and approved.
